Mob costume quirks

Wear either the hood or the suit and the cat picks up the mob's habits:

Cat Enderman HoodBlinks to a nearby spot now and then
Cat Creeper HoodNow and then swells up hissing and flashing… and only puffs out smoke
Cat Skeleton HoodRattles as it walks
Cat Spider HoodClimbs walls
Cat Zombie HoodSmolders in the sun (no damage)
Cat Slime HoodHops now and then
Cat Shulker HoodFloats up into the air now and then
Cat Wither HoodWeakens nearby monsters and cures itself and its owner of wither

Dress Up Cats is a Minecraft mod for NeoForge. This site is generated from the mod's data.
